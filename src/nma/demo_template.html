Keys: 1 to 5 pick a step, space plays or pauses, arrow keys step through time.

Parameters: NavIC L5 chain from ISRO's 2023 proposal (32-bit tags, 128-bit keys, 48 s key period, 96 s disclosure delay, Ed25519 root key); Galileo OSNMA (40-bit tags accumulated to 80 bits, 30 s subframes). The NavIC message layout is modelled, since ISRO's spec is not public. Orbits are circular Keplerian, pseudorange noise 1.5 m.

Replay site: Gurugram, about 13 km south-west of IGI. Signed time: local Roughtime-style server, ±0.5 s radius.
